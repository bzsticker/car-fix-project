Complete user interface layouts, wireframes, and design guidelines for the **Den Modify Management System** have been fully updated to **V3** based on the official brand identity.

## Spec V3 Highlights

1. **Den Modify Brand Guidelines**: Complete shift from neon/cyberpunk aesthetics to stealth matte black bases (`#0B0B0B`), rich charcoal surfaces (`#111111`), and high-performance crimson red accents (`#C40000`).
2. **Comprehensive Screens (24 Screens)**: Exhaustive ASCII structural wireframes detailing:
   - Owner, Admin, Technician, and LINE OA Customer dashboards.
   - POS Dashboard & Checkout Terminal (with live barcode scanning logic).
   - Inventory Dashboard & Product Detail Sheets (multi-branch stock view).
   - Immutable Stock Movement ledgers & Branch Stock Transfer sheets.
   - Digital Invoice / Receipt detail sheets.
   - Warranty Certificates ledger & Claim review tickets.
   - Staff clock-in/out Attendance logs.
   - Customer management modules (Customer List, Customer Detail, Customer Timeline).
   - Vehicle registry modules (Vehicle List, Vehicle Detail).
   - Workshop tracking modules (Job Detail, Job Timeline).
   - Sales proposals modules (Quotation List, Quotation Detail).
   - Performance analytics (Interactive Reports Dashboard).
